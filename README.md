# Growth Audit — Calculator Plus

**Live:** https://bterzic123.github.io/calculatorplus-flow-rebuild/

Five ideas to unlock more revenue from the traffic Calculator Plus already has, each rebuilt as a
working screen in a clickable prototype of the iOS onboarding and paywall. The prototype uses
Calculator Plus's own design: the brushed-metal keypad, the blue display, the plan tiles, the
tracking screen. Only the order changed.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Offer the one-time 14,99 € when they close | ARPU +10–15% |
| 02 | Let them calculate before the price | CR +10–20% |
| 03 | Ask about tracking after the paywall | CR +10–15% |
| 04 | Name what they calculate on the paywall | CR +15–20% |
| 05 | Put your 4.8 and your press logos on the paywall | CR +10–15% |

Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps —
not measured lift for Calculator Plus.

**Grounding.** Every price, rating, press logo and feature line comes from Calculator Plus's own
App Store listing (US, German and French storefronts), its App Store screenshots and its in-app
screens. The phone screens are available in English, German and French; store prices stay as
published in euros.

## Run it locally

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Then open http://localhost:8000/. Add `?open=proto` to land straight on the prototype.
